# Handbook — security-06 (chapter 069)

## Overview

This chapter of the platform handbook covers security-06 at the level the
whole company needs. It is LONG on purpose: the handbook is the one
place where prose is allowed to be thorough, and every section below
exists because someone asked for it in a review at some point.

## Section 069.1 — What this chapter is

The chapter describes how we work, not how any single service works.
Where a service disagrees with the handbook, the service's own runbook
wins for that service, and a PR to this chapter should follow so the
handbook stops disagreeing with reality.

## Section 069.2 — The checklist nobody skips

1. Read the whole chapter before filing a question.
2. Check the FAQ at the bottom.
3. Ask in the handbook channel with a link to the exact section.

## Section 069.3 — Historical notes

Earlier revisions of this chapter were written when the platform was
smaller; paragraphs below preserve the older guidance for context.
The older guidance mentions older tools, older queues and older
dashboards, all of which were replaced. The replacement notes were
added in later revisions and the retired tooling stayed documented
for one more year, which has now also passed.

## Section 069.4 — FAQ

- Q: Does this chapter apply to refunds? A: No; money-flow behavior is
  owned by the billing and orders services, not the handbook.
- Q: Where are the runbooks? A: In each service repository.
- Q: Why is this chapter so long? A: Because every section was asked
  for. Adding more is easy; removing any is a meeting.
