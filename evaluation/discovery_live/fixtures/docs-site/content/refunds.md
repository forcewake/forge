# Requesting a refund

This help-center page explains how CUSTOMERS request a refund. It is
marketing/support content: no service reads it and no code consumes it.

## When can I ask for a refund?

You can request a refund for up to 30 days after your order expires.
Open the order in your account page and choose **Request refund**.

## Do refunds need approval?

Some refunds are reviewed before the money moves back to your card. If
your request needs a review we will email you; reviews usually finish
within two business days. The support team cannot see the internal
rules that decide which requests are reviewed.

## How long does the money take to arrive?

After a refund is issued, card refunds take 3-5 business days and bank
transfers take up to 10 business days. Instant rails are not available
for refunds.
